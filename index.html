01 — About

India's largest student-led Economics and Business mega-fest.

XAVAGE brings ambitious students from leading schools across the country onto one floor for two days of pitching, negotiating, trading and defending ideas under pressure. It is run entirely by students, and it is run for a cause.

02 — By the numbers

One fest, in four numbers.

01 — Reach

Delegations from across India

XAVAGE runs as an inter-school platform. Leading schools from around the country send teams to one campus for one day, which is why a result here carries weight outside your own corridor.

05 — FAQ
Who takes part?

Students from schools across India. XAVAGE runs as an inter-school platform and past editions have drawn more than 34 schools and over 400 participants.

How does a school register?

Write to the organising committee at xavage26@tisb.ac.in and your school will be sent the registration form along with event allocations.

Where does the money go?

XAVAGE is a charity fest. Past editions have supported causes including healthcare initiatives and wildlife conservation, and the cause for the current edition is announced at the closing ceremony.

How do we sponsor?

Write to the organising committee at xavage26@tisb.ac.in and they'll walk you through partnering with XAVAGE 26'. Current sponsors are listed on the Our Sponsors page.

Where is it held?

On campus at The International School Bangalore, on 28th October 2026.

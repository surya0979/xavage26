Platinum
HMG Stones

Founded in 1986 as Hindustan Marble and Granite and transformed by Anand Reddy into Bangalore's leading natural stone house, HMG Stones imports marble, granite and onyx from over 50 countries and runs a fully automated processing facility producing 300,000+ slabs a month. The client list spans homeowners, builders, designers and major commercial projects — including Trump Towers (NYC), UB City, Vikas Soudha and The Chancery Hotel — backed by showrooms in Bangalore and New York.

Gold
Kaari Naari

Founded in 2024 by Samriddhi Shah, Kaari Naari empowers marginalized women in Varanasi through dignified livelihoods. Each tote is handcrafted from khadi, supporting rural weaving communities, and stitched by women from marginalized communities. Women receive training in financial literacy, entrepreneurship, and negotiation, transforming sewing machines into pathways toward sustainable income and self-reliance. Featuring digital artwork of Varanasi by local artists, each bag celebrates one of the world's oldest living cities while supporting its women and cultural heritage.

Silver
Emversity

Bengaluru-based edtech building industry-aligned training and placement pipelines into university curricula. Live across 40+ campuses with 4,500 learners trained and 800 placed, backed by a $46M Series A from Premji Invest and Lightspeed.

Begani Group

A leading Nepali conglomerate with a diverse presence across aluminium, uPVC, plastics and oil refining, while championing professional sports through the Nepal Kabaddi League — combining industrial excellence, entrepreneurial ambition and a commitment to driving Nepal's economic growth.

Rescript

Rescript is a sustainable stationery brand founded by two Christites, offering a range of recycled paper products — from A4 copier paper and notebooks to pens and pencils — that help reduce climate change. All their products are made purely from waste and discarded paper, not trees, and actively contribute to four of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. So far, Rescript has recycled 1,000 tons of paper, saving 18,000+ trees and 400 lakh litres of water, and preventing 13 lakh kilos of carbon emissions. It currently caters to corporations including DMart, J.P. Morgan, Hero MotoCorp, LIC and ICICI Prudential.

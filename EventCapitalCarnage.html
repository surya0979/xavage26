Capital Carnage

Numbers don't lie. Neither does the scoreboard.

Think you can out-think, out-buzz, and out-bid the best economics minds from other schools? Capital Carnage is an inter-school quiz that puts your understanding of economics and finance to the real test. No rote memorization, just sharp thinking under pressure. Battle through three escalating rounds: a fast-paced quiz to open, a high-voltage buzzer round where speed and nerve decide who survives, and a Jeopardy-style finale where bold wagers can make or break your standing. Only three teams make it to the final bell. Assemble your team of three to four, sharpen your instincts, and find out if you've got what it takes to walk away on top.

How it works

Round 1 is set. Rounds 2 and 3 are coming soon.

Round 1 — Market Open

1 hour, written
Entering: ~10 teamsAdvancing: 6

Every team is seated with a bubble sheet — no external materials, devices, or notes once the round starts. 25 questions run across three segments, projected for everyone at once: 10 straight MCQs on core economics and finance (5 points each, no negative marking), 10 chart/graph-interpretation MCQs (5 points correct, –2 incorrect), and 5 written finance questions with working shown (5 points, marks awarded for working).

The 6 highest-scoring teams advance. A tie for the cutoff is broken by a sudden-death buzzer round on current affairs — one wrong answer eliminates a team.

Rounds 2 & 3 — Coming soon

Details closer to 28th October

Rounds 2 and 3 crank up the speed and the stakes — think live buzzers and bold wagers — with the finer details revealed as we near 28th October.

Rules

What you can argue about on the day.

Eligibility & team composition

Each team must have 3–4 members; a team with fewer than 3 present is withdrawn and forfeits its slot. A school may register more than one team, provided each has 4 distinct members — no student may be on two teams. No substitutions once a team has checked in for Round 1; if a member becomes unavailable mid-event, the rest continue with fewer players. Each school designates one team coordinator as the single point of contact.

What you may and may not bring

No phones, smartwatches, notes, or external reference material of any kind at any point in any round. Devices used for Round 1 are provided by the organising committee and are the only permitted electronics. A scientific calculator, pencil, eraser and sharpener are permitted and required for some questions — a graphing calculator is not permitted, and no calculators are provided on the spot.

Timing

Round 1: 45 sec / 1 min / 2 min per question across the three segments. No submission before time runs out scores 0, no negative marking.

Timing for Rounds 2 and 3 will be announced once the format is revealed.

Conduct & disqualification

Teams may not communicate with or receive assistance from anyone outside their own members during active play. Spectators must stay silent during active question-reading and answering in Rounds 2 and 3 — audible coaching gets a warning, a repeat costs the offending school 5 points. The moderator's ruling on correctness is final and not open to floor debate. Prior knowledge of questions or use of prohibited materials is disqualification from the entire event. A team disrupting play or answering out of turn gets a warning, then a 2-point cut, then a 5-point cut on repeat offenses.

Tiebreakers

A sudden-death buzzer round with rapid-fire economics and finance questions. One representative from each tied team comes up to answer; the first wrong answer eliminates that team from contention.
